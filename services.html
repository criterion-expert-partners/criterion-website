Core Service

Active, case-specific sourcing of expert witnesses matched to your specialty, jurisdiction, and case theory. A Criterion search specialist reviews your case, searches the Panel, runs conflict and Daubert screening, and delivers pre-vetted expert profiles within 24–48 hours. We don't query a database — we conduct a search.

Best for
  • Trial attorneys approaching expert disclosure deadlines
  • Cases requiring an active practitioner rather than a career witness
  • Multi-specialty cases where different experts are needed simultaneously
  • Situations where a prior expert has become unavailable or conflicted

Peer Review & Record Analysis
Full service detail →

Independent clinical review of medical records, case files, and prior expert reports by a specialist matched to the condition, standard of care, or practice area at issue. Produces a written opinion. No testimony required — evaluation only.

Best for
  • Early case assessment before committing to full expert engagement
  • Evaluating plaintiff expert opinions in defense matters
  • Settlement negotiations requiring independent clinical documentation
  • Cases where determining merit before client intake is needed

Service 03
Independent Medical Examinations
Full service detail →

Complete IME coordination — physician selection matched to the injury or condition, scheduling, examinee logistics, and report turnaround tracking. Board-certified physicians with experience appropriate to the claim at issue. Criterion manages the process from selection to final report delivery.

Best for
  • Personal injury and workers' compensation defense
  • Cases where plaintiff medical claims require independent evaluation
  • High-value injury claims where ongoing treatment is disputed
  • Cases requiring future care cost assessment

Service 04
Bill Review
Full service detail →

When billed medical charges drive claim value as much as — or more than — underlying liability, bill review is essential. We review the case type and all file specifics, then identify and engage a bill review professional whose experience matches the claim. Frequently deployed alongside peer review or IME for a complete damages picture.

Best for
  • High-bill PI and med mal cases where charges appear inflated
  • Cases involving hospital liens or third-party billing disputes
  • Defense matters where damages reduction is a priority
  • Cases paired with IME where both injury and billing are disputed
Request bill review
Service 05
Site & Facility Inspections
Full service detail →

On-site inspection and forensic evaluation for construction defect, premises liability, and workplace safety litigation. Inspectors are matched by structure type, defect category, jurisdiction, and building code standards applicable to the matter. Coordination includes scheduling, site access management, and inspection report delivery.

Best for
  • Construction defect — residential, commercial, or mixed-use
  • Premises liability cases involving structural or code issues
  • Workplace safety and OSHA-related litigation
  • Environmental site assessment matters
