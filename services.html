Our Services

End-to-end expert witness placement and coordination, tailored to your case needs.

Expert Sourcing & Vetting

We source vetted expert witnesses across all major litigation disciplines. We handle the heavy lifting — background verification, conflict checking, litigation history review, and reference calls — so you get qualified candidates fast.

Case Coordination & Management

Once your expert is engaged, we manage all logistics — scheduling, document coordination, invoicing, and status updates. You get one point of contact for all expert-related matters, freeing your team to focus on strategy.

Expert Selection & Comparison

We don't send one resume — we present 2-3 vetted candidates with a detailed comparison sheet covering credentials, experience, availability, and our expert recommendation. You pick the best fit for your case strategy.

Conflict & Admissibility Review

We conduct thorough conflict checking and review expert backgrounds for prior Daubert challenges or adverse rulings. Our vetting protects you from admissibility issues before they become problems in litigation.

Specialized & Niche Expert Placement

Need a rare specialty or an expert in a specific geography? We source specialists across all disciplines — from engineering to finance, technology to medicine — and get them engaged on your timeline.

Rush & Emergency Placements

Deposition next week? Expert cancellation? We prioritize rush placements and can often deliver vetted candidates within 24-48 hours when timelines demand it.
